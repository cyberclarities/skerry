# Commercial Licensing

Skerry is free to use, modify, and share for noncommercial purposes under the
[PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE). Commercial use needs a paid
license from CyberClarities.

## Free, no license needed

- Charities and nonprofit organizations, including churches and faith-based
  ministries
- Individuals using Skerry for personal study, research, testing, or a home lab

These come straight from the license text, not from a side agreement. If your
organization fits one of them, you are covered regardless of how it is funded.

## Paid license required

- A for-profit business running Skerry to manage its own risk register
- A consultant, MSP, or MSSP using Skerry to deliver work to paying clients
- Hosting Skerry as a service, or bundling it into a product you sell
- Any other use with an anticipated commercial application

If you are not sure which side of the line you fall on, ask. We would rather
answer a question than have someone guess wrong.

## Getting a commercial license

Contact CyberClarities at **consult@cyberclarities.com** with your organization name, how
you plan to use Skerry, and roughly how many people will use it. Pricing is set
per organization.

who already has one of those copies keeps the MIT rights to that copy. The
PolyForm Noncommercial terms apply to versions released from this change on.
