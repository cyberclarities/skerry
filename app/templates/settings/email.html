Service Desk tickets
Adds a Service Desk card with a Send button to each risk page.
Use a category dropbox address from SolarWinds (Setup › Service Desk › Categories), so the incident lands in the right category with its default assignee. The email's subject becomes the incident title and the body its description. If SolarWinds uses an allowed list, {{ smtp_from or "the sending address" }} must be on it.
Covers risks created by hand and from Spotlight. Existing risks are sent with the button on the risk page.
What goes in the ticket
Always included: risk ID, rating, residual score against appetite, status, action owner, risk owner, due date, source, who logged it, and a link back to Skerry.
