Risk Action Request: {{ risk.risk_id }}

An action request asks the CIO and CISO to mandate a course of action on this risk. Everything the register already knows (scores, controls, treatment, owner, vulnerability findings) is pulled in automatically. The fields below belong to this request, not the risk record, so they are not stored; review and edit them, then generate. You get two documents: a one-page Risk Action Request laid out as Background, Issue, Options, and Recommendation, with a mandate block for both executives to sign; and a Supporting Evidence document with the full risk mechanics, vulnerability detail, treatment options, and RACI.
